  **"NEVDIS V2"** and **"Rego API"** as work already in active

**It is not established whether these are the same work described

initiatives.** This document does **not** resolve that question and does
not assume either interpretation. Per the governance rule quoted in Section
0, this ambiguity is itself a blocker: a product cannot be authorised while
it is unclear which discovery-deck item (if any) it corresponds to, or
whether duplicate delivery effort is being planned under two different
names in two different documents.

**This flag must be resolved — by the accountable owner(s), not by this
document — before Stream 2 (or any pptx-deck item covering the same
apparent scope) is authorised to proceed.**
