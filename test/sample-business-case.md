# Business Case — Regional Depot Scheduling Tool

Status: draft for assurance
Owner: Delivery Manager, Fleet Operations

## Executive Summary
This project will deliver a new scheduling tool for regional depot staff to
reduce manual rostering effort and improve on-time departures. Total
estimated cost is $250,000 over two stages, funded from the FY26 operations
improvement budget.

## Business Justification
Manual rostering currently takes approximately 6 hours per depot per week
across 12 depots. The tool is expected to reduce this substantially and
improve customer satisfaction.

## Benefits
- Reduced rostering admin time (hours/week per depot)
- Improved customer satisfaction

## Financial Case
Stage 1 (design and build): $140,000
Stage 2 (rollout and stabilisation): $40,000
Total project cost: $180,000

Contingency has not been separately identified; it is assumed to be included
within the stage estimates.

## Risks
- Staff availability for user acceptance testing during rollout is not yet
  confirmed with depot managers.

## Governance
Project Board: Fleet Operations Director (Executive), IT Delivery Lead
(Senior Supplier), Regional Operations Manager (Senior User).
